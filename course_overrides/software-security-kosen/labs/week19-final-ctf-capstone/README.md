# Week 19 — Final team practical

**Software Security 2 (02005094) · one 150-minute exam · date, time and room to be announced.** The KOSEN final exam period is 8–16 March 2027 (the calendar says dates may change). Your instructor will post the exact date, time, room, allowed aids and where to submit in an official notice.

You work in your existing project team of 2–3. The 150 minutes are: 10 minutes of instructions, 130 minutes working on **four stations** (25 marks each, 100 marks in total), and 10 minutes to check that your submission arrived. [The four stations and the marking scheme](ctf.md).

All 100 marks count as Technical and make up **15%** of your course grade. Your project report, code and video are marked separately and are due **before Week 19**; project questions are answered in Weeks 16 and 20. Where a task gives a flag, the flag is useful proof, but full marks also need your own fix, one example that should work, one that should be refused, and a clear explanation. Leaderboard points are only for fun and give no marks.

You will get a starter pack from your instructor on the day. **How to prepare:** redo the weekly student tasks that match each station: Week 10 (Station A), Week 11 (Station B), Weeks 12 and 13 (Station C), and Weeks 14 and 15 (Station D).
