# Week 9 — Midterm individual practical

**Software Security 2 (02005094) · individual work · 150 minutes in total:** 10 minutes briefing, 130 minutes on the tasks, 10 minutes to check your upload.

There are **four stations (A, B, C, D)**. Each station is worth 25 points, so the practical is out of 100. Those 100 points count as **12% of your course grade**. The full task sheet, what to hand in and how it is marked are here: **[practical brief](ctf.md)**.

**What to revise:** Week 1 and Week 2 (threat models and scanner results), Week 3 (password hashing), Week 4 (SQL and command injection) and Week 6 (login tokens and "who owns this object" checks). Week 5 is not on the practical. The Week 7 mock practice is the best warm-up.

**What to expect on the day:**

- Your instructor hands out a small exam pack with the starter files. You do not need a CTF account, and there are no personal flags to find.
- A flag on its own does not earn full marks. You get marks for your own fix, for showing that it works, and for explaining why.
- The date, room and permitted aids (including AI and internet rules) come in the official exam notice. Follow that notice.

The older challenge bank from earlier versions of this course is not part of this exam.
